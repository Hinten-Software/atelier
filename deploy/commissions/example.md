size: 700

## Theme
The last light of a winter afternoon indoors.

## Subject
A kitchen table by a window: a white jug, three lemons, a dark bottle, a folded cloth.

## After
Chardin's still lifes: few things, set low and close on a plain ledge against a warm dark ground; forms built in
thick, broken, dry paint over thin darks; soft edges, and one or two crisp lights.

## Technical
The jug's lit side is the one true white; the bottle and the room behind hold the blacks.
